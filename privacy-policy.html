Privacy Policy

Offroad Racing 4x4 (Android package com.offroadracing4x4.app)
Last updated: 22-11-2026

This policy explains what information the game Offroad Racing 4x4 ("the game") handles, who handles it and what choices you have. The game is published by Rajendra Chourasiya ("we").

Summary

  • The game has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads.
  • We do not run our own servers for the game and we do not receive or store your personal information.

Information kept on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: coins, unlocked levels and vehicles, upgrades, paint choices, settings and statistics such as distance driven. This information never leaves your device. It is deleted when you use "Reset progress" in the game's settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall the game.

Information collected for advertising

The game uses Google AdMob, an advertising service from Google LLC, to show banner, full-screen and rewarded video ads. When ads are requested or shown, Google's software in the game collects and processes information directly. We do not see this information about individual players.

Information Why Google uses it
Advertising ID of your device Choosing ads, limiting how often an ad repeats, measuring ads, detecting fraud
IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) is worked out Showing ads suited to your region, security
Device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version) Delivering ads in a format your device supports
How you interact with ads (shown, tapped, video watched to the end) Measuring ads and paying out rewards
Diagnostics (crash and performance data of the ads software) Keeping the ads service working

Google explains how it uses this information here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland). When required, the game asks for your choice about personalised ads before any ad is requested. Until you answer, no ads are loaded.
  • Advertising ID. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads (the wording varies by phone). With the ID deleted, ads are not personalised.
  • Rewarded videos are optional. You choose whether to watch one in exchange for coins, an extra life or an early unlock.
  • Play offline. The game works without an internet connection. No ads are shown and nothing is sent while offline.

Permissions

The game uses internet access and network state (to load ads), the advertising ID (for ads) and vibration (for feedback when you crash or land). It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help where we can; note that we hold no player data ourselves.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases and handles no payment information.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep no personal information about players. Game progress stays on your device until you delete it as described above. For information held by Google for advertising, use Google's own controls at My Ad Center or the links in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
